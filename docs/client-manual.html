1. About this manual

This manual shows you how to apply for a PRDF business loan and manage it once it is funded. It follows the screens in the order you will meet them, so you can read it straight through the first time and afterwards open it at whichever section you need.

Sections 2–4Before you apply — what you need, checking that you qualify, and creating your account
Sections 5–9The application itself — the five steps, your documents, and the declaration you sign
Sections 10–13After you apply — tracking progress, and your loan account once funds are released
Section 14What your loan costs, explained
Section 15If something goes wrong
Section 16Acceptance and sign-off
A note on the figures. The amounts in the screenshots come from a test application for R750 000 over 12 months. Your own figures will differ. Everything the portal quotes you before approval is indicative — Section 14 explains why, and what can change.

Troubleshooting

If you get stuck, contact your PRDF loan officer. If you have not been assigned one yet, use the contact details on the PRDF website. Section 15 covers the problems people hit most often, and many can be solved without waiting for help.

2. Before you begin

Gather all ten documents before you start. You cannot submit your application until every one of them is uploaded, and stopping halfway to find a missing document is the most common reason applications are abandoned.

2.1 What you need

DocumentWhat it must be
ID DocumentCertified copy of the director's or applicant's identity document
Proof of AddressRecent proof of business or director address
Company Registration (CIPC)Your CIPC company registration certificate
Tax ClearanceSARS tax clearance or tax compliance status document
Bank StatementsThree months of business bank statements (you may upload several files)
Financial StatementsLatest annual financials or management accounts
Vendor QuotationsThree quotations for what you are funding (several files allowed)
Central Supplier Database (CSD) ReportYour CSD registration report
Purchase Order / Short-Term ContractThe order or contract itself, including validity. Contracts may not exceed three years
Trade ReferenceA reference from a business organisation or trade reference
File formats and size. Only PDF, DOC and DOCX files are accepted, and each file must be 5 MB or smaller. Photographs and scans saved as JPG or PNG will be refused. If you only have a paper copy, scan or photograph it and save it as a PDF before uploading — if the scan comes out larger than 5 MB, scan it again in black and white or at a lower resolution.

2.2 Also have ready

  • Your CIPC registration number, in the form 2021/123456/07
  • Your SARS tax pin
  • Your average monthly revenue, years trading, and number of employees
  • Your business's physical address
  • A South African mobile number and an email address you can access

2.3 Practical points

Browser. Any current version of Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. The portal works on a phone, but uploading is far easier on a computer where your files already are.

Time. As a guide, allow around 10 minutes once every document on the checklist is to hand — it will take longer if you are still gathering them. This is an estimate, not a limit: you can save and come back at any point, so you do not have to finish in one sitting.

3. The PRDF website

Everything in this section works without an account. Start at https://prdf-lms.vercel.app.

3.1 The loan calculator

The calculator gives you an indicative price before you register anything. Drag the sliders, or click the figure and type directly into it.

  • How much do you need? — R250 000 to R3 000 000
  • Over how many months? — 1 to 36 months

It then shows your indicative monthly instalment, total repayment, and estimated interest.

These figures are indicative only. They are a guide to help you plan, not a quotation and not an offer of credit. Your actual rate is set after assessment, and your approved amount and term may differ from what you ask for. Section 14 explains what can change and why.
Read the rate notice first. The figures are quoted at the cheapest rate we offer, because at this point we know nothing about your business. The notice above them says Prime + up to 10.50% — that is the ceiling. Your actual rate is set after we assess your application. This is not a credit approval or an offer.

Whatever you set on the calculator is carried through to your application, so you do not have to enter it twice.

3.2 Checking whether you qualify

Check Eligibility opens a checklist of PRDF's criteria, grouped into developmental impact, ownership and control, and registration and compliance. Tick each one your business meets. A counter at the bottom shows your progress.

4. Eligibility outcomes and your account

4.1 The two outcomes

When you click Check My Eligibility you get one of two answers.

This self-check is not recorded and is not sent to PRDF. It is there to save you time. You can also skip it entirely and apply directly.

4.2 Creating your account

Apply Now, or Create Account & Apply on the result page, opens registration. All five fields are required.

FieldRule
First nameCannot be blank
Last nameCannot be blank
Phone numberA South African mobile number — 0 or +27, then 6, 7 or 8, then 8 digits. Spaces and dashes are fine
Email addressMust be a valid address, and one you can access
PasswordAt least 8 characters

If anything is wrong, the message appears directly beneath that field and the cursor jumps to it. Clicking Create Account & Apply takes you straight into the application — there is no separate confirmation email to wait for.

Use an email address you can actually open. It is the address you sign in with, the only way to reset a forgotten password, and where PRDF writes to you about your application. An address belonging to someone else, or one you no longer have access to, will lock you out of your own account.

5. Signing in

5.1 If you forget your password

Type your email address into the Email address field first, then click Forgot your password? If you click it with the field empty you will be asked to fill it in.

PRDF emails you a reset link. Open it, choose a new password, enter it a second time to confirm, and the portal confirms the change. The link works once and expires after a short time — if it has lapsed, request another from the sign-in page.

You are signed out everywhere after a reset. That is deliberate: if someone else had your old password, changing it ends their access too. Sign in again with the new password on each device you use.

5.2 Staying signed in

You stay signed in if you close the tab or the browser and come back later, so you will not lose a draft that way. Behind the scenes your sign-in is renewed about once an hour while you use the portal; you will not notice this. If a renewal cannot be completed — most often because the connection dropped — you are returned to the sign-in page, and nothing you have saved is lost. Logout, at the top right of every page, is the reliable way to end your session, and the one to use on a shared computer.

5.3 Where you land

  • If you have never applied, you go straight into the application form. Home and Status only open once you have an application to show.
  • If you have applied before — including an unfinished draft — you land on Home.

Be specific here. An assessor reads this. "Funding a confirmed purchase order to supply 40 tonnes of farmed abalone to an export buyer, covering feed, grading and cold-chain packaging" tells them far more than "expansion", and helps your application move faster.

9. Step 4: Documents, and saving your work

All ten documents are required. You cannot move on until each one has a file.

9.1 Uploading

Click a zone to open the file picker, or drag a file onto it. Each file uploads immediately — there is no separate save.

  • View opens the stored file in a new tab.
  • Remove deletes it and frees the slot.
  • Only PDF, DOC and DOCX are accepted — see Section 2.

9.2 Saving and coming back later

Your work saves itself about a second and a half after you stop typing, and again each time you click Continue. The header tells you: Saving…, then ✓ All changes saved.

You can also click Save & finish later on any step. To pick up where you left off, sign in and either use Resume your draft on Home or Status, or just click Applications.

One application at a time. You can only have one unfinished application open. Opening the form again resumes the one you have rather than starting a new one, so you cannot accidentally create two. Discard draft in the header deletes it and its files permanently.

10. Step 5: Review and submit

Check everything before you submit. After this point you cannot edit your application yourself.

To change something, use ← Back, or Edit loan details on the right-hand panel to jump straight to Step 3. Submit Application stays greyed out until every document is present.

10.1 The declaration

Submit Application opens PRDF's consent declaration: twelve statements covering data privacy (POPIA), policy acknowledgements, and terms and conditions.

This is a legal declaration. Read it rather than clicking through. Among other things you confirm that your information is true and accurate, that you understand approval is not guaranteed, that you must insure any assets bought with the loan, and that repayment will be collected by debit order. You cannot proceed without agreeing to all twelve. Your answers are stored with your application.

10.2 What happens next

  1. Your application is saved and your declaration attached.
  2. Its status changes to Submitted.
  3. You are taken to the Status page, where you can follow its progress.

If the timeline looks empty, nothing is wrong. While your application is being assessed it passes through several internal stages that the timeline does not yet show, and it can appear as though no progress has been made. Trust the status label at the top right of the card, not the circles. This is known and is being fixed.
If we need more from you, the status changes to Information Requested and an Action required panel appears. Check your notifications for what is needed, then upload it on the Documents page. Your application waits until you do.

14. What your loan costs

There are three charges, plus a penalty if you pay late.

ChargeHow it worksWhen
InterestCalculated daily on the full amount you borrowedOver the term
Initiation feeA flat R1 000Once-off
Management fee3% of the amount you borrowOnce-off
PenaltyOnly if you pay late — see 14.3Only when late

14.1 Your interest rate

Your annual rate is the Prime lending rate plus a margin based on your risk grade, which PRDF sets after assessing your application. Prime is currently 10.5%.

Risk gradeMarginAnnual rate
Low5.0%15.5%
Moderate6.5%17.0%
High8.5%19.0%
Worst10.5%21.0%

The portal quotes you at the cheapest grade before assessment, which is why it says From 15.50% p.a. Your actual rate can only be the same or higher, and appears on your loan account once your loan is booked.

14.2 How you repay

One payment at the end of the term. Interest builds up daily on the full amount you borrowed and is settled together with the capital on your maturity date. Your schedule therefore shows a single instalment rather than monthly ones. The fees are deducted from the money advanced to you, so you receive your loan amount less the initiation and management fees.

For example: borrow R750 000 over 12 months at 17%, and you would receive R726 500 (R750 000 less R23 500 in fees) and repay R877 500 at maturity (R750 000 capital plus R127 500 interest).

14.3 Paying late

There is no grace period. A penalty starts accruing the first day you are late, calculated on your capital plus interest at 2% per 30 days. It is worked out in a straight line and never charged on top of earlier penalties.

14.4 Before approval, nothing is final

Everything the portal shows you before approval is indicative. Your final rate follows assessment; your final amount and term may differ from what you asked for; and approval depends on document verification, affordability and PRDF's assessment.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

  • Can I change my application after submitting? No. Contact your loan officer.
  • How long does a decision take? The site indicates five working days, depending on assessment.
  • Is the quoted figure what I will pay? No — it is indicative until approval. See Section 14.
  • Can I apply for more than one loan? One application at a time. Others appear in your Status list.
  • How do I pay? By debit order. Not through the portal.
  • Is my information safe? Your documents are stored privately and opened only through short-lived secure links. How we handle your data is set out in the POPIA consent you signed.
